
## The joke

136 characters (gate: 1 to 199). Why this line: it is a real joke with an unexpected turn — the open-plan office was meant to make people talk, and the punchline is that it made everyone stop talking. It rings true about office life, the wording is tight, and the take is fresh.
